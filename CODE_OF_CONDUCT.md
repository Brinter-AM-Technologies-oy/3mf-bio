# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is about getting facts right and admitting when they are not. That requires
people to be able to say "this is wrong" without it becoming personal, and to hear it
without becoming defensive.

**Expected:**

- Disagree with claims, methods and code. Cite sources when you do.
- Say when you do not know. `kind="unverified"` is a first-class outcome here, not a
  failure state.
- Accept correction. The changelog records a case where a value copied from four vendors
  turned out to describe the wrong chemical species; finding such things is the point.
- Assume competence and good faith in others.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Asserting facts you have not checked, or defending them after they are shown wrong.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately using the *Report a vulnerability* button on the
Security tab, which is visible only to maintainers, or by opening an issue if
the matter is not sensitive. Reports will be reviewed and responded to.
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation, for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Maintainers who do not uphold this in good faith may face the same consequences.
